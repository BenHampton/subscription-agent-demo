# Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation

- Customers may cancel their subscription at any time.
- Upon cancellation, access continues until the end of the current
  billing period. No partial refunds are issued for unused time
  within a billing period.
- Annual subscriptions may be cancelled, but refunds for the
  remaining months are only available within the first 30 days
  of the annual term.

## Refund Eligibility

- Monthly subscriptions: Full refund available if requested within
  7 days of the charge date. After 7 days, no refund is available.
- Annual subscriptions: Full refund within 30 days of purchase.
  After 30 days, no refund. No partial refunds.
- Duplicate charges: Always eligible for a full refund regardless
  of timing. The agent should verify the duplicate in the billing
  system before processing.
- Service outages: If a verified outage exceeded 4 hours, customers
  are eligible for a prorated credit (not a refund) for the
  affected period.

## Refund Amounts

- Refunds up to $50 may be processed automatically by the support
  agent without manager approval.
- Refunds between $50 and $200 require the agent to document the
  reason and may be processed automatically.
- Refunds over $200 must be escalated to a human manager for
  approval. The agent should NOT process these directly.

## Credits vs Refunds

- Credits are applied to the customer's account balance and used
  toward future invoices. Credits do not expire.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically
  take 5-10 business days to appear.
- For service issues, prefer credits over refunds as they retain
  the customer relationship.

## Non-Refundable Items

- Setup fees (one-time charges during onboarding)
- Data migration services
- Custom development or consulting hours
- Third-party integrations billed through our platform
