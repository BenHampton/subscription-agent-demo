# Frequently Asked Questions

## Billing & Payments

### How do I update my payment method?

Navigate to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods. You can
add a new card and set it as default. The old card will be used for
any pending invoices already generated.

### Why was I charged after cancelling?

If you cancelled after your billing date, the charge was for the
current period which was already in progress. Your access will
continue through the end of that period and no further charges
will occur.

### Can I get an invoice for my records?

Yes. All invoices are available in Account Settings > Billing >
Invoice History. You can download PDF invoices for any past charge.
For custom invoice formats or purchase orders, contact support.

### What happens if my payment fails?

We retry failed payments three times over 7 days. During this time
your account remains active. If all retries fail, your account is
downgraded to a limited free tier. You can update your payment
method at any time to restore full access. No data is deleted for
30 days after downgrade.

## Account Management

### How do I add team members?

Go to Settings > Team > Invite Members. Enter their email addresses
and select their role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer). They will receive
an invitation email. Invitations expire after 7 days.

### Can I transfer ownership of my account?

Yes. The current owner can transfer ownership to any Admin-level
team member from Settings > Team > Transfer Ownership. This action
requires email confirmation from both parties.

### How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Data > Export. You can export all your data in
JSON or CSV format. Export processing takes up to 24 hours for
large accounts. You will receive an email with a download link
when the export is ready.

## Technical

### What browsers are supported?

We support the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and
Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported.

### Is there an API?

Yes. API access is available on Professional and Enterprise plans.
Documentation is at docs.example.com/api. Rate limits vary by plan:
Professional gets 1,000 requests/day, Enterprise is unlimited.

### Do you offer SSO?

SSO via SAML 2.0 is available on Enterprise plans only. We support
integration with Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, and OneLogin.
Setup requires coordination with our team — contact your account
manager or support to begin configuration.
